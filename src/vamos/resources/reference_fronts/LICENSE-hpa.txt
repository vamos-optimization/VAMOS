Third-party reference fronts and data: Namura's human-powered aircraft (HPA) benchmark

The packaged files hpa201_0.csv to hpa901_2.csv are the approximated Pareto fronts that
Nobuo Namura published with the HPA benchmark, the reference points of the IGD+ values of:

  N. Namura, "Single and Multi-Objective Optimization Benchmark Problems Focusing on
  Human-Powered Aircraft Design", Evolutionary Multi-Criterion Optimization (EMO 2025),
  https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-96-3506-1_14 (arXiv:2312.08953).

Source: https://github.com/Nobuo-Namura/hpa, directory igd_reference_points/n=4, commit
3524115 (HPA201-0.csv to HPA901-2.csv). They are the fronts of the penalized problems
(HPAM0L-l) with n = 4 wing segments. The values are unchanged; only the header row and the
row-index column were removed.

The HPA problems of VAMOS (vamos.foundation.problem.hpa) are a port of the same repository's
hpa/problem.py and hpa/designer.py, and its airfoil tables, hpa/airfoil_info/DAE11.xlsx to
DAE41.xlsx, are copied in vamos/foundation/problem/hpa/airfoils.py.
