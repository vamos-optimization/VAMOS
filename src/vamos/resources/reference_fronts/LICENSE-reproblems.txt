Third-party reference fronts: Tanabe and Ishibuchi's RE problem suite

The packaged files below are the approximated Pareto fronts published by
Ryoji Tanabe and Hisao Ishibuchi with their RE problem suite:

  R. Tanabe and H. Ishibuchi, "An easy-to-use real-world multi-objective
  optimization problem suite", Applied Soft Computing 89 (2020) 106078.

Source: https://github.com/ryojitanabe/reproblems, directory
approximated_Pareto_fronts, commit 7876b4e465eac381a256e461d1310b8bb2b92846.
The values are unchanged; only the column separator was changed from
whitespace to a comma.

- re24.csv: reference_points_RE24.dat
- re31.csv: reference_points_RE31.dat
- re32.csv: reference_points_RE32.dat
