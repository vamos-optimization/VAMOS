Third-party reference fronts: jMetal

The packaged files below are reference fronts of the jMetal framework:

  A. J. Nebro, J. J. Durillo and M. Vergne, "Redesigning the jMetal
  multi-objective optimization framework", GECCO Companion 2015, 1093-1100.

Source: https://github.com/jMetal/jMetal, directory resources/referenceFronts,
commit 1e4b83cae6. The values are unchanged. DAS-CMOP8 and DAS-CMOP9 leave out
two rows each, which another row of the same file dominates.

- lircmop1.csv to lircmop14.csv: LIRCMOP1.csv to LIRCMOP14.csv
- dascmop1.csv to dascmop6.csv: DASCMOP1.csv to DASCMOP6.csv
- dascmop7.csv to dascmop9.csv: DASCMOP7.3D.csv to DASCMOP9.3D.csv
- mw9.csv to mw13.csv: MW9.csv to MW13.csv
- mw4.2d.csv, mw4.3d.csv, mw8.2d.csv, mw8.3d.csv, mw14.2d.csv and mw14.3d.csv:
  MW4.2D.csv, MW4.3D.csv, MW8.2D.csv, MW8.3D.csv, MW14.2D.csv and MW14.3D.csv
- binh2.csv, golinski.csv, osyczka2.csv, water.csv and car_side_impact.csv:
  Binh2.csv, Golinski.csv, Osyczka2.csv, Water.csv and CarSideImpact.csv
